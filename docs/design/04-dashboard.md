# 04 — Dashboard

## Top bar (this panel)
Title « Tableau de bord » / "Dashboard" + descriptor « Votre mois en un coup d'œil ». Right: month selector pill (‹ Mai 2026 ›, 38 px, raised surface), global search pill (250 px, placeholder « Rechercher… »), user pill.

The chevrons step one month; the label between them opens a month-and-year picker — a 236 px popover on the shared select-popover treatment (field fill, hairline, popover shadow, radius 14) holding a year stepper (‹ 2026 ›, tabular) over a 3×4 grid of 32 px standalone month cells. The selected cell takes an iris 12 % fill, iris label and 600 weight, so selection is never carried by color alone. The browsed year is popover state: paging it and dismissing without choosing leaves the dashboard's month untouched, and re-opening starts back on the month in force. Stepping is the fast path for the neighbouring month; the picker is what keeps a month a year back from costing a dozen clicks.
