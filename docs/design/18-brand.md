# 18 — Brand: logo, texture, installer splash, startup screen

Mockup: `#panel=logos`, `#panel=textures`, `#panel=splash`. Ticket #202. Read
`00-design-system.md` first.

## 1. The mark « Plan »

The grid plan of a bastide, a new town built around a central square. The square, the only
coloured cell, is what stays at home.

### Geometry (24-unit grid)
- Tile: 24 × 24, corner radius 5.3 (22 % of the side).
- Nine squares, each 4 × 4, corner radius 0.8, at x and y ∈ {4.5, 10, 15.5} (gap 1.5, margin
  4.5).
- The centre square (x 10, y 10) is the accent; the other eight are the glyph colour.

```svg
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 24 24">
  <rect width="24" height="24" rx="5.3" fill="TILE"/>
  <!-- for y in 4.5, 10, 15.5; for x in 4.5, 10, 15.5 -->
  <rect x="X" y="Y" width="4" height="4" rx=".8" fill="GLYPH"/>
  <!-- centre: x=10 y=10 fill="ACCENT" -->
</svg>
```

### Colours
| Use | Tile | Glyph | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| On a dark ground (dark theme, splash) | `#ECEDEF` | `#111214` | `#7FA6FF` |
| On a light ground (light theme) | `#17181A` | `#FFFFFF` | `#2B5CD0` |
| App icon (taskbar, dock, installer, favicon) | `#17181A` | `#FFFFFF` | `#7FA6FF` |
| Monochrome (print, single-colour contexts) | none | current colour | current colour at 45 % |

Inside the app the tile is `primary`, the glyph `onPrimary`, the centre `accent`, so the mark
follows the theme.

### Sizes and clear space
- Minimum 16 px. At 16 and 20 px draw the same geometry; do not simplify.
- Clear space: one cell (4 units) on every side.
- Never: a gradient, a shadow, an outline, a rotation, a second coloured cell, the mark
  without its tile on a busy ground.

### Lockup
Mark + « Bastide » in Geist 600, tracking −0.02 em, capital B. The name's cap height equals
the height of two cells and a gap (9.5 units); the gap between mark and name is 8 units.
Sidebar: mark 20 px, name 14/600. Auth: mark 20 px, name 16/600. À propos: mark 44 px, name
16/600. Splash: mark 64 px, name 26/600.

follows the theme.

## 2. Texture « tomettes »

Definition and the five allowed places: `00-design-system.md` §12.

Implementation: one `CustomPainter` in `core/widgets` that tiles the path at 1 px stroke with
the theme's texture colour (white or black at 7 %), and a wrapper that applies the fade
(radial for auth and empty states, vertical for the sidebar, none for the goal banner and the
À propos header). No image asset. It replaces `frame_texture.dart`, which is deleted.

## 3. Installer splash (Windows, Velopack)

The installer shows one static image while it copies the app, at its pixel size times the
display scale, and draws its own progress as a 12 px strip across the image's bottom edge.
The image can hold no changing text and no animation.

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | **560 × 320 px** (unchanged from the current splash). PNG, opaque; SVG source beside it. |
| Files | `frontend/windows/installer/splash.svg`, `splash.png` |
| Ground | `#111214`; tomettes at 9 % white, faded by a radial gradient of the ground colour centred at 50 % / 46 %, opaque to 28 % of the radius, clear at 100 % (radius 62 % of the image) |
| Mark | Plan, 64 px tile, centred horizontally, top edge at y = 84. Dark-ground colours. |
| Name | « Bastide », Geist 600, 26 px, tracking −0.02 em, `#ECEDEF`, centred, baseline at y = 190 |
| Sentence | « Vos données restent sur cet ordinateur. », Geist 400, 13 px, `#A3A7AE`, centred, baseline at y = 216. French only: the installer cannot translate it. |
| Progress zone | The bottom 12 px (y 308–320): a `#25272B` track painted in the image. The installer fills it. |
| Progress colour | `--splashProgressColor "#7FA6FF"` in `tools/package_app.py` (was `#8B8CF9`) |
| Forbidden | Gradient fills, coloured glow, version number, slogan, the word « Installation… » |

Text is converted to outlines in the SVG so the PNG does not depend on an installed font.
A light variant exists in the mockup for reference; only the dark image ships.

macOS (`.dmg`) and Linux (AppImage) have no splash; they get the new icon only.

## 4. Startup screen (in the app)

Shown in the app window while the local engine starts, and when it fails.

- Ground `bg`, tomettes with the auth radial fade. One centred column: Plan mark 64 px (theme
  colours) · « Bastide » 26/600 · « Vos données restent sur cet ordinateur. » 13
  `textSecondary` · 24 px · a status line 12 `textTertiary`.
- Status line: « Démarrage du moteur local… » with a 14 px spinner before it. Translated.
- Failure: the status line becomes a `negative` status text with the reason, and a secondary
  « Réessayer » sits under it. Version mismatch (debug builds): the existing message, with
  « Relancer » and ghost « Continuer quand même ».
- It follows the theme preference of the last signed-in user; dark when unknown.

## 5. Removed from v1
The iris-gradient tile with ascending crenellated bars, the gradient lockups, the iris splash
and its `#8B8CF9` progress colour, the film grain and the luminous hairline, the radial glows
of the auth screens.
